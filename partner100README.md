# The Partner 100 Starter

Find your first 25 dream partners: the people who already have your customers.

## How to use it
Pick the AI you already use.

- **Claude.** Make a Project. Paste `SKILL.md` into the project instructions. Open a chat in it and say: **start my Partner 100**.
- **ChatGPT.** Make a custom GPT, or start a chat and paste `SKILL.md` as the first message. Then say: **start my Partner 100**.
- **Hermes.** Put this folder in your digital employee's skills folder. Then say: **start my Partner 100**.

It needs an AI that can search the web. Give it your website address when it asks.

## What you get
A table of 25 names you approve, sorted into tiers, each with the honest way in and a first touch written only for them. Then the ten to work first.
